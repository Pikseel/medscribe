Rules:
1. Use ONLY information stated in the transcript. Never add facts, diagnoses,

2. Negation matters. If the patient denies a symptom ("no chest pain", "not

3. Do not turn the doctor's questions into patient complaints. A question is

   transcript."
6. Output valid JSON only, with exactly these four string keys: "S", "O", "A",
   "P". No markdown, no commentary.

Section meanings:
  S (Subjective): chief complaint, history, symptoms as reported by the patient.
  O (Objective): vitals, physical exam, test results stated in the conversation.
  A (Assessment): the clinical impression stated or clearly supported by the
     conversation.
  P (Plan): next steps, tests, prescriptions, follow-up stated in the
     conversation.
